Photos

Campus Life

International Students Organization

This photo captures a memorable moment from the International Students Organization Game Nigh event at Buffalo State University.

We had Bingo games, prizes, food, and activities representing cultures from around the world. It was an incredible opportunity to connect with other international students and share our unique perspectives.

Student Leadership Engagement Program

The SLE Program banquet was a celebration of student leadership achievements at Buffalo State. As a student leader in Student Leadership and Engagement, I helped organize this event to recognize outstanding student leaders contributions.

This banquet brought together student leaders from various organizations and clubs to celebrate accomplishments and foster connections across campus communities.

Rockwell Road on Campus

Rockwell Road is one of the main pathways through Buffalo State University's campus. This photo was taken during the fall semester.

This road connects several academic buildings. It's particularly scenic during autumn when the foliage changes color.

There's nothing like Milk tea and some toasts in the leisure day. This simple ritual includes tea and some snacks that remind me of home.

Visiting University at Buffalo

This photo was taken during a visit to the University at Buffalo campus. A friend studying there invited me to explore the campus and attend a guest lecture event.

Videos

TopGolf in Las Vegas

My first experience playing at TopGolf in Las Vegas. Despite being a complete rookie at golf, I had an amazing time trying out this unique sports entertainment complex.

Buffalo State in Winter

Buffalo winters are famous for their snow, and this video shows our campus covered in a beautiful white blanket. The campus transforms completely during the winter months.

This was my first experience with such heavy snowfall. Despite the cold, the snow-covered campus created a peaceful and serene atmosphere that was quite different from my home country.

New Year's Eve at the Sphere

Celebrating the New Year in Las Vegas was an unforgettable experience. This video captures the incredible fireworks display at the Sphere, one of the newest landmarks on the Las Vegas Strip.

The combination of fireworks and the Sphere's massive LED display created a spectacular light show to ring in 2024. The energy of the crowd and the scale of the celebration made this a truly memorable moment.
